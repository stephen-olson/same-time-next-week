Flying Home is a weekend dance event dedicated to:
The Best Music
We feature internationally recognized swing jazz bands that make you want to move—while honoring the legacy of the jazz greats who shaped this music. Every note celebrates the past and energizes the present.
The Best Instructors
Our instructors are not only exceptional dancers—they’re passionate, inclusive teachers who support dancers of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels. Whether you’re brand new or deeply seasoned, you’ll find inspiration and growth here.
The History
The Triangle has long been a crossroads for music, dance, and Black cultural traditions. We’re part of a region with a rich history of jazz, swing, and social dancing—and we want to celebrate the people who have carried those traditions forward. Our dance family is spread across the globe, and sometimes it's only at events like this that we’re reunited. We want you to feel like you've flown home—welcomed with open arms, familiar faces, and shared joy on the dance floor.
The Belief that Jam Circles Are for Everyone
When the music hits just right and the circle forms, magic happens.
