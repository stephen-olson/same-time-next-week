Now Open: Our New Food Bank at Durham
We’re excited to share that our new Durham Branch is now open to the public — and it’s a big step forward for our hunger relief network and the communities we serve!
With leading support from our friends at Food Lion Feeds, the new 64,000sq ft Food Bank at Durham is purpose-built to help meet the rising need for support in our community.
