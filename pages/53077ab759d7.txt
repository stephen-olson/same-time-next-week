Arrival and Shift Times
• The morning shift runs from 8:30am-12pm, and the afternoon shift from 1:00pm-4:00pm.
• Volunteers must register online at www.orangehabitat.volunteerhub.com.
• Please arrive five to ten minutes early for your shift and stay for the entire time.
• When you arrive, please make a nametag and check in with Habitat staff
• Before beginning work, Habitat staff members will lead an orientation to review important information about safety and construction tasks for the day.
…
• You may want to bring a water bottle and a snack. Habitat will provide a water cooler and cups.
• Please leave all valuables at home.
What to wear
• Comfortable clothes that you do not mind getting dirty or covered in paint.
• Closed-toed athletic shoes or work boots are required—no sandals or Crocs.

What if I wake up the day of my shift and it's raining?
Depending on the stage of construction, we may or may not be able to work. Habitat will update the weather hotline at (919) 932-7077 option 1 by 7:15am in case of weather-related cancellation.
…
How old do I have to be to volunteer at the worksite?
Volunteers must be at least 16 years old to work on new home construction. All minor volunteers must have a waiver signed by a parent or guardian on file prior to volunteering.
What else should I know about volunteering on a Habitat construction site?
Habitat relies on volunteers like you to help keep our building costs low. Thank you for being ready to work hard, try something new, and pitch in wherever needed as the shift unfolds. Orange Habitat welcomes groups to come out for a build day. Past builds have included groups of employees, students, or faith partners. If you would like to bring your group to build with Habitat, please fill out this interest form.

Regular Crew
Our Regular Crew is a dedicated group of 50+ individuals who volunteer on our construction sites consistently on Wednesdays, Fridays, or Saturdays. Email Julia at jhenn@orangehabitat.org for more information.

AmeriCorps
AmeriCorps is the federal agency connecting individuals and organizations to tackle the nation’s most pressing challenges. Most AmeriCorps positions serve 10.5 months with hands on work on our new home construction, home repair, or volunteer management teams.
