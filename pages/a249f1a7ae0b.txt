Volunteer
Volunteers are critical to the success of Oak City Cares multi-services center for people experiencing homelessness. The relationships between our volunteers and guests are key to making sure our guests feel welcome at Oak City Cares.
If you’re new to volunteering or want to learn more about ways to get involved at Oak City Cares, start by signing up. Once you are ready to move to the next steps and sign up for a shift, you will need to create an account and complete a few qualifications prior to volunteering.
Please consider joining our fantastic team of volunteers at Oak City Cares! Check out all our volunteer opportunities or browse the weekday and weekend services via the buttons below.
