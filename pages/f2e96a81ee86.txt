Join TLC to explore the ecology of our fall forests!
Enjoy the quiet of autumn as we look for hidden habitats, explore the life cycles of forest creature and look for signs of natural and human impacts on forest life. This 3 mile moderate hike features some steep inclines and variable terrain. This is a free event, but pre-registration is required:
