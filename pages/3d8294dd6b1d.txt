Join Rewild NC on Saturday, Nov 14 from 1-3PM for an Invasive Plant Removal Workday at Third Fork Creek. Park at The Woodcroft School: 1307 W Woodcroft Parkway.
Volunteers will continue to clear Chinese Privet from the woodlands along the creek. With hand saws and loppers we will cut the privet, drag it into piles, and apply herbicide to the cut stumps with daubers to prevent regrowth.
CLOSED TOED FOOTWEAR AND LONG PANTS ARE REQUIRED ATTIRE FOR THESE EVENTS. Any volunteers who show up wearing shorts and/or sandals will be turned away for their own safety. Long sleeves and tall socks are also recommended to protect from exposure to poison ivy, brambles, and insect bites.
Please bring a reusable water bottle to stay hydrated. Tools and gloves will be provided but we encourage volunteers with their own labeled pruners and saws to bring them along.

Join the Forest Hills Neighborhood Association to participate in a litter cleanup around Forest Hills Park (1639 University Dr).
Volunteers will meet at the picnic shelter near E Forest Hills Blvd and Beverly Dr. There is some parking available in a small lot off E Forest Hills, and more in the lot off University Dr. The group leader will send more details by email 1-2 days prior to the event.
Gloves, safety vests, litter grabbers, and trash bags will be provided. Please help reduce waste by bringing your own reusable water bottle. Wear sturdy shoes or boots, long pants, and dress for the weather. Supervised children of all ages are welcome.
