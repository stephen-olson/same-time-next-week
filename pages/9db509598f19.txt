Available Shift Times: Shifts can be set by the volunteer anytime between 10:00-6:00 Thursday-Saturday.

Transportation Volunteer
These volunteers take dogs and cats to and from their vet appointments, pick up animals from other shelters/fosters, pick up donated supplies, and pick up medications for the animals. Volunteers can choose to drive their own car or the Paws4ever van (as long as they are at least 25 years old).
Approximate Training Time: 45 minutes
Group Volunteer Opportunities
Paws4ever offers group volunteer opportunities for community members who want to support animals in need but may not have time to volunteer regularly. If you’d like to organize a group volunteer event, please reach out to the Paws4ever Volunteer Manager at volunteer@paws4ever.org.
