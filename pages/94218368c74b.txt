Volunteers
Volunteers make an incredible difference every single day at Orange County Animal Services. From walking dogs and socializing cats to helping at events and supporting adoptions, our volunteers help provide enrichment, comfort, and care to the animals who need it most.
Whether you want to spend time with animals, support the community, or gain hands-on experience in animal welfare, there’s a place for you at OCAS.
Getting Started
Before applying, please review the information below about volunteer requirements, training, and opportunities.
…
Ages 12 and Under
Are not eligible to volunteer inside the shelter facility. However, there are still many ways to help animals outside the shelter environment.
Volunteer Expectations & Hours
Volunteer shifts are available Monday through Saturday.
After completing 50 volunteer hours, eligible volunteers may gain access to Sunday shifts and additional opportunities.
…
Each shift is approximately 2 hours long
All volunteers are expected to assist with basic shelter tasks, including:
Laundry
Dishes
Spot cleaning animal areas
…
During this hands-on session, volunteers will:
Learn shelter procedures and safety protocols
Work alongside a volunteer mentor
Become familiar with volunteer roles and expectations
Gain hands-on experience with animals
…
Commmunity & Support
Not every volunteer role involves directly handling animals. Community volunteers help support the overall operation of the shelter and outreach efforts throughout Orange County. Opportunities may include:
Surgery Day Ambassador
Clerical Assistance
Laundry & Dishes
…
Animal Transport
Emergency Shelter Support
These roles are critical to keeping daily operations running smoothly and helping us connect with the community.
Fostering
Not ready to volunteer onsite? Fostering is another incredibly impactful way to help.
Fosters provide temporary homes for animals who may need:
A break from the shelter environment
Recovery time for medical care
Socialization
Bottle feeding or neonatal care
…
You do not need to be an active volunteer to become a foster.
Learn more about fostering at: www.orangecountync.gov/fosterpets
Dog Adventures
One of our most popular opportunities is participating in Dog Adventures. These programs give dogs a chance to get out of the shelter environment, explore the community, and spend quality one-on-one time with individuals who are 18 or older.
Dog Adventures opportunities includes:
Dog Day Out
Hikes with Hounds
…
and more to come!
These outings help dogs reduce stress, build confidence, and show off their personalities outside of the shelter. These individuals also help by taking photos, sharing information about the dogs, and providing valuable feedback about how the dogs do in different environments.
Questions?
Please contact volunteer@orangecountync.gov with any questions about volunteering or fostering opportunities.
