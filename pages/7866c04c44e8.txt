One Day Clay: Winter Candleholders, Saturday November 21st
$75.00
Saturday, November 21, 6:30-8:30pm
Instructor: Anna Schroeder
Location: Sculpture & Handbuilding Studio
Skill Level: All Levels Welcome
Make an adorable votive holder with Anna! In this beginner-friendly 2-hour class, students will create a winter scene: start with a pinch pot and add the seasonal adornments of your choice. Students will learn basic sculpting techniques including pinch pots, texture-making, and making little critter…
Ages 18+ The workshop fee covers one participant and includes clay, bisque firing, and glaze firing of one piece. The firing process takes 3 weeks; Claymakers staff will contact you by email you when your piece is available for pickup. Tools will be provided for use.
