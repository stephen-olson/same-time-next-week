ABOUT THE DURHAM TAPROOM
Hi-Wire Brewing’s journey began in the summer of 2013 in the heart of Asheville, NC’s South Slope Brewery District, but the 2018 opening of our Durham, NC, location at the Golden Belt Campus marked the first of Hi-Wire’s growing list of taprooms outside of Asheville.
