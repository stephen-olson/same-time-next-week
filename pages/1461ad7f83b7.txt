Opportunity Name:
2026 Holiday Express Event Volunteers
Description:
For the month of December, Pullen Park is transformed into a winter wonderland where guests can experience crafts, holiday lights, a visit with Santa and Mrs. Claus, and more!
Duties and Impact:
By volunteering at this event, you can help enhance the magic of the holiday season for kids and their families. Volunteers will be assigned the following zones and respective duties upon arrival:
Arctic Avenue: Visitors will participate in a variety of carnival-themed games and activities at the DJ’s dancefloor. This zone involves demoing activities, explaining rules, and motivating participants.
Cocoa Court: Visitors will create souvenirs to bring home, mail a letter to Santa, and find hidden characters around the park. This zone involves crafting, storytelling, and providing instructions to families.
Glistening Glen: Visitors will aim for a high score or take the perfect family photo. This zone involves keeping game stations tidy, timing activities so everyone gets a turn, and encouraging family participation.
