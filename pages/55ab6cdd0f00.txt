Saturday Socials
Monthly Saturday Night Potluck and Community Gathering
Dec 5, Jan 9, Mar 13, Apr 10, May 8
Every month we invite everyone to gather together on a Saturday evening for a shared meal followed by opportunities for fun and connection around our campus. We hope you can join!
Read more below for different offerings:
Potluck @ 5:30 p.m. in Founders Hall
Join us for a community dinner!
We’d be grateful to have you sign up to bring an item to share here, but you are more than welcome to just bring yourself. We’ll provide the potato latkes & sour cream.
Would you join our potluck posse? We are grateful to people who commit to providing three dishes throughout the year to ensure we have a hearty mix of dishes that include vegetarian and kid-friendly options. Sign up here to help.
Activities from 6:30 - 8:00 p.m.
Games in 201 Peace Hall
All ages are welcome for a family friendly game night! We’ll put out our collection of games and invite you to bring your favorites along to share.
All games must have PG content (no adult version of Cards Against Humanity).
Hanukkah Party
Following dinner, a Hanukkah party featuring candle lighting (bring your own menorah or use one of ours), dreidel spinning, song singing, and an exciting game of UUFR Family Feud.
Song Swap in Music Room
Bring your music, stories, poems, or jokes to share and play together! For more information, contact Bill MacMillan: bill.macmillan@ gmail.com
Sign Up for Childcare for NOW Children 11 and Under!
Parents can sign up children 6 and under for childcare from 6:30 – 8:30 p.m. each month here. There are 15 spots available.
Childcare will be held in the Ocean Room (Peace Hall 101). No meal will be provided so please enjoy the potluck beforehand as a family.
Future Saturday Socials
Saturday Socials are truly planned to be events for all ages.
At Saturday Socials, we plan to have a participatory musical event as well as intriguing and inspiring films for adults and mature teens on a variety of subjects.
