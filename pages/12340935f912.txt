Sunday Salsa Social
Sun, Nov 29 · 6:30 PM EST
TDS Space, 2603 S Miami Blvd, Durham, NC, US
Salsa and Bachata with sides of Merengue, Cha, and Kiz. Now in our 15th year.
We are in the large studio in the back.
The Social starts with basic Salsa lesson at 6:30 and dance is from 7:15 to 9:30. Informal dress. Singles, couples and children welcome.
$10. $8 for students and military with ID. Children 16 and under free and welcome. Not every DJ has a credit card reader and when they do may charge $1 extra so best bring cash.
We play 40-50% Salsa, 35-45% Bachata and the rest Cha Cha, Kizomba, Merengue, Cumbia.
DJs are:
First Sunday - DJ Abuelo Salsero, Jack Wolf
2nd- DJ Salsa Mike. Michael Delucia
3rd Sun- DJ Pedro Paz
4th Sun- DJ Monte Alban, Eliuth Cruz
5th Sun, when happens- Guest DJ
If you are new to TDS it can be hard to find as there is no sign, but it is next door to Research Triangle Dental which has a large sign. It is in the building at the back of the parking lot on the left- back studio.
1 attendee
