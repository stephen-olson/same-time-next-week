How does this work?

Running Routes
We have options! You can choose from the following routes or choose your own adventure. You can download the routes below from Strava. If you are not familiar with the area, please print a map to take with you, or be prepared to follow the crowd
1-mile (route goes through Durham Central Park to W Morgan St, up Morris St and back)
3-mile (route goes to Duke Park and along Ellerbe Creek Trail from Club Blvd to Trinity Ave)
4-mile (route goes through downtown, around Duke East, and Trinity Park)
6.4-mile (route goes down to Duke Chapel and back)
