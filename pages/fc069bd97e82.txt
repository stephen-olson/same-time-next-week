Blount Street Volunteer FAQs
What do volunteers do?
Volunteers help steward our one-acre site so we can advance our mission to connect and nourish our community through regenerative agriculture here in Downtown Raleigh.
Our volunteers get down and dirty, build community and learn about sustainable practices of regenerative agriculture. They also support site preparation around events and other programming. What if inclement weather is predicted?
In the event of inclement weather (i.e. severe thunderstorms, heat advisory), we will cancel/reschedule.
We will make every effort to communicate cancellation of workdays ASAP.
If you are the leader of a large group that has been scheduled for a work day, we’ll notify you directly through email if cancellation is necessary and will make every attempt to reschedule for a later date.
Where is the Farm's Blount Street location?
Address: 800 N. Blount Street, Raleigh, NC 27604
GPS Location: 800 N Blount St. 27604 (Corner of Franklin & Blount Streets)
We are located next to Standard Beer + Food and the Person Street Plaza.
Where should I park?
We encourage you to take an eco-friendly approach including walking, biking, riding-sharing, etc. There are also public transit options available. If you must drive, consider carpooling.
Please refrain from parking in the Person Street Plaza lot since our neighboring businesses rely on these spaces for their customers.
Street parking is available along Blount, Franklin & Person Streets (note posted parking restrictions).
How do I sign up?
Check out the events section of our website for regularly scheduled volunteer opportunities and subscribe to our email volunteer alerts.
For large group workdays, learn more here.
For all volunteer inquiries, email us at volunteer@raleighcityfarm.org.
