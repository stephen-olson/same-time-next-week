Stitch n’ Bitch – Steel String’s weekly craft night at the taproom!
Bring your own creative project – knitting kit, stitching project, sketch/coloring book, lego set, or whatever portable art of your choice and craft with us over a drink. Open to all – novices and hardcore “knit-wits” alike.
The hangout is free to attend and everyone simply covers their own cost of beer, wine cider, kombucha, soda, tea, or a non-alcoholic beer from the bar.
*This is not an organised class. Everyone will need to bring their own project to participate or use the pens + coloring books, origami and friendship bracelets supplies provided
Grab a seat, and get knitty with it!
Follow Steel String Brewery on Instagram or check website for any cancelations or updates.
