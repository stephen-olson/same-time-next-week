ADMINISTRATION & SPECIAL EVENTS
Support the Food Shuttle with day-to-day office tasks and special events. Volunteers may answer phones, greet guests, help with mailings and projects, or represent the Food Shuttle at festivals, farmers’ markets, college campuses, and community events.
BACKPACK BUDDIES
Volunteers can help pack bags for delivery or hit the road to deliver them to local sites.

FOOD SHUTTLE FARM
Volunteers help with seasonal farm tasks like harvesting, planting, weeding, packing, and greenhouse work. No experience needed, just a willingness to get your hands dirty!
GROCERY BAGS FOR SENIORS
Volunteers help pack monthly grocery bags with fresh produce, non-perishable foods, and healthy snacks. Volunteers can also deliver the bags to senior housing communities in Wake and Durham counties.
