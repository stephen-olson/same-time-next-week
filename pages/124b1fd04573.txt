About us
The Durham Community Improv Jam is a chance for experienced improvisers and total newcomers to come together and play! At these free events, we perform improvised scenes and play improv games like those you may have seen on shows like “Whose Line Is It Anyway?”
No audience, no pressure, no experience needed! Whether you’re a seasoned professional or brand new, come jam with us and hone your comedy craft in a positive and supportive environment. All events are FREE and open to everyone!
