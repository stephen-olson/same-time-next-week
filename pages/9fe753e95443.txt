Thank you for your interest in the Volunteer Program at the SPCA of Wake County!
As we work toward our mission of transforming the lives of pets and people through protection, care, education, and adoption, we need volunteers to help us. If you love pets and want to help those who don't have homes, we might just have the perfect volunteer opportunity for you.
Volunteer opportunities at SPCA Wake include something for almost every pet lover. The main roles include Butlers, Matchmakers, General Cleaning and Events:
Canine Butler, Feline Butler, Small Pets Butler — Butler roles include hands-on responsibilities such as cleaning bedrooms and condos, potty breaks, enrichment, and positive social interactions with pets.
Feline Matchmaker, Canine Matchmaker — Matchmaker roles include responsibilities that require guest interaction, positive communication, getting to know our pets and conducting meetings between guests and pets.
General Cleaning Volunteers — General Cleaning roles are heavily based on back-of-house duties i.e. laundry, dishes and other supportive tasks.

Long Term/Emergency Foster of Owned Pets
This role reports to our Community Impact Department, and differs from other volunteer and foster roles.
When pet families are in crisis or facing temporary hardship, having a long-term foster to temporarily care for displaced pets can prevent pets from entering the shelter system and instead help families be reunited so pets can return to where they belong — home.
These foster situations could last as long as 3-6 months, possibly longer depending on the needs of the family.
