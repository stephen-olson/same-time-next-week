Thank you so much for your interest in volunteering with us!
A Place at the Table truly could not operate without all of our amazing, committed, and caring volunteers. Our volunteers do almost everything in our cafe, so thank you for being a part of this community!
PLEASE NOTE: Due to our increased volume of volunteers, all volunteers will be limited to 3 hours of volunteering per week. We always want to center community and be a space of connection and relationship building. It is our hope that implementing a volunteer limit will allow us to do so.
Group Volunteering: Because we have a high volume of volunteers, all group volunteer opportunities must be coordinated in advance. If your group is interested in volunteering, we’d love to hear from you! Please contact Kay at admin@tableraleigh.org to learn more about availability and scheduling.
There are 3 ways to volunteer:
1. Volunteer for your meal!
We ask that folks volunteer for an hour in order to receive a meal. Walk-ins welcome.
2. Join the weekly volunteer team
We are currently recruiting weekly volunteers! If you’re interested in joining the weekly team, please email Kay, our Volunteer and Admin Coordinator, at admin@tableraleigh.org.
Sign up for a 15-minute volunteer information session with Kay here!
3. Schedule a volunteer shift
Volunteer when you can! Use the link below to sign up for a shift!
…
When you arrive, you’ll head to our check-in station where you’ll meet your Team Lead for the day! They’ll train you and assign you tasks throughout your shift.
We welcome all volunteers to try our delicious food! Please plan to eat either before or after your shift, unless your Team Lead tells you to feel free to eat in the middle of your shift.
We do have a locked storage room to keep your belongings. However, it’s always encouraged to leave personal belongings at home or in your car if you’re able.
Contact the cafe If you are unable to make your shift (see cancellation policy).
2. Volunteer roles
Our volunteers do so many things in the cafe to help us be successful! Volunteers do things like deliver food and drink to tables, clean tables, roll silverware, wash and dry dishes, greet customers, and so much more!
It’s important that you come ready to jump in and learn something new! Our staff Team Leads will train you and put you in different positions throughout your shift.
3. How to dress
Wear comfortable clothing and remember it may get a little dirty!

4. Late and cancellation policy
We are a full service restaurant that relies heavily on volunteers! If you can no longer make your shift, please simply remove your name from the volunteer platform where you signed up. If it’s a last minute cancellation, please email Kay, our Volunteer and Admin Coordinator, at admin@tableraleigh.o…
5. Youth volunteers
Youth must be at least 15 to volunteer.
6. Group volunteering
We do not have the capacity to bring in large volunteer groups. However, we’d be happy to block off multiple shifts for you so your group can split up and volunteer in smaller groups! Email admin@tableraleigh.org to inquire!
7. First-time volunteers
If it’s your first time volunteering with us, please check-in at the volunteer station when you arrive.
8. Questions
If you have ANY questions, please contact Kay, our Volunteer and Admin Coordinator, BEFORE signing up at admin@tableraleigh.org.
