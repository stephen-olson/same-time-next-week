Folky Friday
Second Fridays from 7-9pm
Room 201 Peace Hall (upper parking lot)
We sit in a circle and take turns playing tunes together out of a songbook assembled by leader Tom Atkinson (you can also bring your own if you provide about 20 copies). You pick the song and kick it off, or ask others to take the lead. Contact Tom Atkinson for more information.
Song Swap
Second Saturdays from 6:30-8pm (this is part of the Saturday Social potlucks, which
sometimes vary on date, so check with leader in advance).
Generally in Founder’s Hall (where the potluck takes place)
We sit in a small circle and each lead a song or two we’ve brought (you can bring copies to share if you provide about 10 copies). For more information contact Bill MacMillan

Founders Hall under the Sanctuary
An “open mic” event where you play or sing a song, read a poem, or tell a story or two for an audience, either solo or with other people. A group of singers and musicians visits a UUFR member at home or in a facility, usually by request of the UUFR Care Committee. Bruce Emery Guitar Salon
Tuesdays 7-9pm
Right corner of the UUFR Sanctuary
Longtime teacher and author of 25+ instruction books shares free guitar training. This includes everything from music theory to ear training. We always make time to play together. All skill levels welcome. For more information contact Bruce Emery.
UUFR Drum Circle
Every fourth Wednesday, 7-8:30pm
Room 201 Peace Hall (upper parking lot)
Stephen Gornick of Drum Together provides the drums and a table full of smaller, exotic percussion instruments and leads a sitting drumming circle for all ages. Previous experience not required. For more information contact Stephen Gornick.

The UUFR choir is always looking for new members! They rehearse Thursday evenings from 7-8:30pm and generally sing at services on the first and third Sundays of the month.
