Durham-Chapel Hill International Folk Dancers
Welcome! The Durham-Chapel Hill International Folk Dancers have been getting together to learn and enjoy international dancing since 1964. The group started in Chapel Hill. We danced in Durham for decades and are back in Chapel Hill.
The dances come from a range of countries, and are done in circles, lines, sets, etc. Partners are not needed, and everyone is welcome to dance or watch and enjoy the music from the Balkans, Greece, the British Isles, Turkey, Israel, Russia, Scandinavia, USA and more. To learn more about Internation…
We usually meet three times a week at the Carol Woods Fitness Center. All group sessions are free and open to the public. No dance experience required. Most dances can be easily joined into. Partners are not necessary.
First-time visitors are asked to sign a liability waiver.
Here is our normal schedule:

This is the main group that has been dancing since 1964. Evenings begin with easier dances. A dance is introduced or reviewed at 8PM.
