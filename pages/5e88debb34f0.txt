Opportunity Name:
2026 Moore Square Winterfaire Event Support
Description:
Help bring holiday magic to Downtown Raleigh! Raleigh Parks is seeking volunteers for Moore Square Winterfaire, a free, family-friendly celebration featuring local vendors, entertainment and seasonal activities. This event will be open to the public December 11 - December 13. Help us make this event…
Duties and Impact:
Volunteers will support hosting this event by assisting with the following duties:
Setup and Preparation: Stage supplies, decorate, and prepare activity spaces.
Activity and Guest Support: Assist with crafts, lawn games, Santa activities, wayfinding and restocking, and running our s’moore station (Saturday only).
Breakdown and Cleanup: Tidy activity areas, pack materials, and assist with park restoration.
By volunteering for this event, you are helping to provide a fun, interactive day for the local community.
Work Environment:
Volunteers will be working outside in the elements. There is the possibility for uneven ground, large crowds, flashing lights, and loud noises. This position will involve extended standing/walking and occasional moving of suppliesThere will be a restroom on site.
