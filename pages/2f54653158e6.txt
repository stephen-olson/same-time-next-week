Volunteering at Piedmont Wildlife Center
PLEASE NOTE: We do not fulfill court-ordered community service hours.
Get involved in a great cause! Help conserve wildlife, build community, and share your passions and interests at Piedmont Wildlife Center.
We can often schedule volunteer work days for clubs, businesses, and other groups. If you are a team lead looking for group opportunities, please fill out the form linked below.
Group Volunteer Interest Form
Service Days
Service days are single-day events focused on maintenance, building projects, and park beautification.
Volunteers for service day events must be 18 or older. Due to the physical demands of volunteer activities and the hazards of working outside, all volunteers must sign a waiver.
Register HERE
HIghway Clean ups
PWC is a part of the Adopt-A-Highway program in North Carolina. Many of our wildlife ambassadors are with us as a result of litter. Sign up to help protect our native wildlife and beautify Durham by becoming a Pumpkin Protector.
Volunteers for highway cleanups must be 18 or older. All participants must sign the AAH Program Agreement on the day of the event. Registration is required.
