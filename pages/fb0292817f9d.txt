Description:
The Warehouse Project takes place inside the warehouse at each of our branches. At times, depending on the weather, this opportunity may also be held outdoors. While supporting the Food Bank in this activity, volunteers may be asked to assist with the following tasks:
Sorting produce and placing it into family-size bags
Sorting donations from retail stores
Cleaning and bagging Capri Sun pouches
Building CSFP boxes for senior citizens
Sorting and inspecting food drive donations from individuals and corporate partners
Cleaning and sorting eggs
Bagging cereal, rice, or pasta into family-size bags
Assisting with other activities, depending on donations received
Reminder: Closed-toe shoes are required at all times while volunteering at the Food Bank.
