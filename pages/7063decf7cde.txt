Weekly Game Locations
Go players currently meet many times each week in the Research Triangle Area of North Carolina.
We welcome and encourage all levels of players, from beginners to high dans.
Mondays in Raleigh: Play Go at the the Sidequest Game Lounge and Bar in Raleigh (Map)
from 6 PM until 10 PM every Monday night.
Popular with Go players, this is a gaming bar & grill.
Tuesdays in Carrboro: We play from 5:30 PM until 8 PM at Lanza's Cafe in Carrboro (Map).
Parking is available and all levels of players are welcome!
Fridays in Durham: One of our most popular meetings is at the Southpoint Starbucks at Barnes & Noble in Durham, from 6:15 PM until 9 PM.. (Map)
Friendly folks, great coffee and loads of books!
Sundays in Durham: We gather at The Boot Room at Rockwood (Map) from 2:00 until 4:30 or so.
This is a spacious spot with fine cuisine and plenty of parking.
Occasional Sundays from 1:00 PM until 5:00 PM at Meeple's Brew in Morrisville, by arrangement (Map).
