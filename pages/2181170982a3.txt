Opportunity Details | Worthdale Holiday Event
Opportunity Name:
Worthdale Holiday Event
Description:
Worthdale Community Center is hosting our annual indoor Holiday Party in the gym, featuring carnival games, arts and crafts, festive food and drinks, and a toy giveaway. Join staff in hosting this event by helping create a warm, joyful experience for families in our community.
Duties and Impact:
Volunteers will support hosting this event by assisting with the following duties:
Carnival Games Support: Help run and supervise various carnival-style games for children and families.
Arts and Crafts Assistance: Guide participants through fun hands-on holiday crafts.
Toy Giveaway Support: Help distribute toys to children during the event.
Food Distribution: Assist staff with passing out food and refreshments to attendees.
By volunteering for this event, you are helping to create a festive, welcoming environment for families to celebrate together.
Work Environment:
Volunteers will be working indoors in a gymnasium setting. There is the possibility for large crowds, flashing lights, and loud noises. There will be a restroom on site.
