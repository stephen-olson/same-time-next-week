Opportunity Details | Mural Painting at Brentwood Park
Opportunity Name:
Mural Painting at Brentwood Park
Description:
Join Raleigh Arts staff as we paint a mural at Brentwood Park. This project is part of an effort to refresh the neighborhood center. The mural will be laid out as a giant paint-by-number, and all are welcome to join regardless of artistic experience. Come join in the fun and get creative!
Duties and Impact:
Volunteers will use brushes, rollers, and ladders to paint designated sections of the mural. By volunteering for this event, you are helping to create an exciting piece of art for the local community to enjoy.
Work Environment:
Volunteers will be working outside in the elements. There is the possibility for uneven ground. There will be a restroom on site.
