Thanksgiving Meal Packing and Delivery Sign Up
On Thanksgiving Day, Raleigh Rescue Mission in partnership with Meals on Wheels will provide meals to those in need in the Raleigh community.
This Turkey Meal Community Event will begin with volunteers gathering to pack meals at Christ the King Presbyterian Church (1417 Clifton Street, Raleigh, NC 27604). Meal distribution will also be at Christ the King Presbyterian (1417 Clifton Street, Raleigh, NC 27604).
We need volunteers to help pack, deliver, and distribute meals. Sign up for the day you would like to volunteer.
You do NOT need to attend volunteer training to help with Thanksgiving and Christmas meals. You only need to attend training if you plan to volunteer onsite at Raleigh Rescue Mission.
