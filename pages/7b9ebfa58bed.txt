Our History
Quest Game Night events were started in March, 2022 by Jason Mathew who hosted a small group from Quest Fellowship Church twice a month to hang out and play games.
That summer, Jason started a Meetup group which combined a Cary board game club that had lost it's leader and Outside the Box board game meetup formerly led by W. Eric Martin, long time editor at BGG and owner of Board Game Beat. The two groups were combined into the Garner Board Games Meetup.
Twice a month, 60-100 tabletop gamers visit Quest for a Friday night full of gaming and friendship.
Over 4 years and 1,800 members later, we have grown to be the most attended board game meetup in North Carolina and have been re-branded to Game Nights @ Quest!
Quest Con is held at Quest Fellowship Church and hosted by members of our Meetup group.
