Duties and Impact:
By volunteering at this event, you can help enhance the magic of the holiday season for kids and their families. Volunteers will be assigned the following zones and respective duties upon arrival:
Arctic Avenue: Visitors will participate in a variety of carnival-themed games and activities. This zone involves demoing activities, explaining rules, and motivating participants.
Cocoa Court: Visitors will create souvenirs to bring home and mailing a letter to Santa. This zone involves crafting, storytelling, and providing instructions to families.
Work Environment:
Volunteers will be working outside in the elements. Volunteers are expected to be on their feet for the duration of their shift. There will be restrooms on site.
