About Pullen Dance
We’re a small, friendly community dance that holds weekly dance socials at Pullen Park Community Center in Raleigh. We also host periodic live performances with bands such as the Triangle Jazz Orchestra.
Adults of all dance skills are welcome to attend. We dance to recorded music, including salsa, cha-cha, rumba, east- and west-coast swing, foxtrot, waltz, and the occasional line dance.
No partner required! We don’t offer lessons, but we welcome newcomers, and many of our attendees are happy to show you a few steps.
Weekly dances + live performances
A portion of the $5 fee for each weekly dance helps us fund live performances with great bands such as the Triangle Jazz Orchestra. The more people who attend our weekly dance, the more often we can host great live music.
A great dance floor
Pullen Park Community Center has one of the best dance floors in the Triangle. It’s a true raised floor so you can dance your socks off and then dance some more.
