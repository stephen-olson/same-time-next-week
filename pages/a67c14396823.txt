Join our partners at Wake Audubon Society for birding in the Museum Park on the second Saturday morning of each month.
Preregistration is required. This program is managed by Wake Audubon. Find more details, register, and direct any questions to them through their Meetup page.
Please note: once registered, you will be provided with more information and any updates by Wake Audubon, not the NCMA.
