About us
Fun, friendly, inclusive dancing. Want to meet friendly people, and get some exercise at the same time? The Carolina Dance Club sponsors a long running (22 years), very popular, social dance every Sunday at the Raleigh Elks Lodge. We focus on swing (lindy hop, west coast swing), ballroom, country so…
Come early and participate in a 55 minute beginner lessons prior to social dancing. No partner or prior experience is necessary to join us for the lesson or social dancing.
The Carolina Dance Club is the offshoot of our DJ and Entertainment company, Music and Dance Productions. Our goal, and passion is to provide outstanding social dance opportunities and instruction for our Triangle NC community. It has been our quest since 1996.
My wife Debbie Ramsey and I met dancing in New York City. We moved to the Triangle area back in 1997, and have made this our home ever since. We are professional instructors, dancers and dj's, and love what we do. Debbie Ramsey, is a US Open Swing Dance Champion, California Hall of Fame Swing Dancer…
Come out any Sunday for a great social dance experience.

EVENING SWING DANCE and AFTERNOON LINDY HOP, BALBOA, CHARLESTON WORKSHOPS with instructor Wesley Boz.

4 PM - BAL-BALBOA SWING: (Beginner-Intermediate level). Know your basic 8 count balboa step? We'll be working on several swingouts to use in your balboa dancing.
