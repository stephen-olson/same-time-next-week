Morgan Street Run Club
Come join us every Monday at the Bend Bar and State of Beer in Raleigh, NC.
Runners/walkers leave @ 6PM with most opting for the mapped out 3, 4, or 5 mile routes, some even choose their own path. After the run, stay and enjoy a beer, food and trivia.
If you are new, make sure you show up to your first run 15 minutes early to register.
Liability Waiver
You knew it was coming… our liability waivers. Please plan to arrive 15 minutes early for your first run to sign our electronic waiver. If you are pushing it on time, here is our online version. Just read it, fill it out, and you’ll be all good for Monday Night Run Club!

Frequently Asked Questions:
What do I need to know about the Monday run?
nOg Run Club meets every Monday on Morgan Street (849 W Morgan Street, Raleigh, NC 27603) starting at 6pm.
If you are new, make sure you show up to your first run 15 minutes early to register. After the run, stay and enjoy a beer. Each week we have trivia, which starts at 7pm. Maps are provided for 3, 4, and 5 mile routes.

Where should I park?
Parking is available on Morgan Street, next to the Bend Bar, as well as the adjacent streets surrounding Trophy Brewing on Morgan Street.
What are the rules of the nOG Run Club?
nOg Run Club has a really large group of runners and needs participants to run safely and courteously; therefore, we need you (the runner) to agree to the following running safety guidelines while participating in the nOg Run Club:
I will run on the sidewalk whenever possible, when not running on the sidewalk, I will run on the side of the road, facing on-coming traffic at all times (the left side for most of the route).
I will wear reflective clothing, accessories, or lights for my safety and the safety of others.
I will stop at all stop signs and stop lights to insure that oncoming traffic yields to me before proceeding across the road.
…
I will cross streets at intersections or official crosswalks, always yielding to oncoming traffic.
