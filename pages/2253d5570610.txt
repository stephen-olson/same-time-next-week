Dungeons & Dragons Adventurers League is a weekly “living" role-playing campaign using the 5th Edition of Dungeons & Dragons. As you participate in adventures, characters earn experience and treasure, leveling up and becoming more powerful. Official Adventurers League characters are eligible to part…
Atomic Empire hosts Adventurers League every week on Thursdays from 6:30pm–10:30pm. The entry fee is $7 per session. Or, players may pay for a monthly membership, available for $17, that includes unlimited play for that month.
If you have never played in the League before, new players can try out their first session for no charge!
To reserve your spot at a table, please visit our Warhorn portal and look for the DDEX games listed. Every table needs a Dungeon Master! As a token of our appreciation, Dungeon Masters receive $10 in store credit for running a game.
Make sure you bring the following:
A set of polyhedral dice (available at Atomic Empire)
A writing utensil and scratch paper
A miniature or small marker to represent your character
A character sheet, if you’d like to complete one prior to playing
