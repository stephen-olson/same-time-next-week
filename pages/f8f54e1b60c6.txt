Thursday, December 10 @ 7:00 pm - 9:00 pm
Jam Session
PineCone’s Old Time Jam takes place on the 2nd Thursday of each month from 7-9 p.m. at Riparian Provision Company (1408 South Saunders Street, Raleigh). This is an up-to-speed, gather-in-a-circle kind of jam. It’s led by great players who work to make sure there’s space for everyone in the tunes.
The leader picks a key (D, A and G are popular) and calls tunes in that key until it feels right to pick a new key and retune. Whether you’re a first-time jammer or a veteran, you’re welcome to learn the tunes and play along, or just listen and observe! However, please note that this is not a “learning” or “slow” jam.
