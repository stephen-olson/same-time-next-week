ERA - Fall Nature Fun Day!
ERA - Fall Nature Fun Day!
November 7, 2026 12:00 pm - 4:00 pm
Confluence Natural Area, 4214 Highland Farm Rd, Hillsborough, NC 27278, USA
NOTE: This event has been rescheduled from Saturday, October 17th due to inclement weather. We will hold the event on Saturday, November 7th. Same time, same place!
Help the Eno River Association celebrate the sights, smells, and sounds of fall on the Eno River! Join us for a FREE, family-friendly day of hands-on fun at the Confluence Natural Area.
Get your hands and feet wet while searching the creek for aquatic macroinvertebrates, make a festive fall-inspired craft, and learn about some of the Eno’s most fascinating “spooky” species.
This event is FREE and registration is NOT required. Drop by anytime between noon and 4pm. Geared toward families with kids, but nature lovers of all ages are welcome.
Restrooms are available on-site. Two accessible parking spaces connect the paved sidewalk to the pavilion. At least one activity will take place in the paved pavilion.
