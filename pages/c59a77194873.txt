What is included in class tuition?
• Weekly class instruction
• 25 pounds of clay (firing included)
• Access to Claymakers’ open studio hours (Tuesday-Saturday, 1-6 pm)
How does your waitlist work?
At the bottom of each individual class page, there is a space to enter your email address to the waitlist. If a space becomes available, everyone on the waitlist is notified at the same time by email. The waitlist spot is filled on a first-come, first-serve basis.
Cancellations are not uncommon, but we can’t guarantee that a space will open up in your preferred class.

What is your class refund policy?
If you choose to drop a class, you will receive a refund of your tuition, excluding a non-refundable $25 registration fee per reserved space, provided that your withdrawal occurs seven days before the start date of the session as a whole (eg, Session 1).
…
Please note that no refunds or credit will be given for missed individual classes within the session.
We reserve the right to cancel or reschedule classes to adjust for enrollment. If a class is cancelled, you will be offered the option of another class or a full refund.
…
If you drop a workshop up to 30 days before the workshop start date, tuition will be refunded except for:
• Workshops under $150: a $45 non-refundable registration fee per space reserved
• Workshops over $150: a $75 non-refundable registration fee per space reserved
No refunds will be made within 30 days of the start of the workshop, once the workshop has started, or for missed sessions. If a workshop is cancelled due to insufficient enrollment, you will be offered the option of another class or a full refund.
