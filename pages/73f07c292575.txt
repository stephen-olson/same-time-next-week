Join us for an evening of Spanish conversation, music, and community at The Bike Library!
Spanish will be the language of the night, but you don’t need to speak Spanish to join. Whether you’re fluent, learning, or simply interested in listening and picking up a few words along the way, everyone is welcome.
Come practice without pressure, meet new people, enjoy music, and try a few Spanish-inspired beverages from the cafe.
