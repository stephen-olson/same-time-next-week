Library Taskmaster!* Community Engagement is bringing the highly popular comedy hit Taskmaster to the library. This adults-only program will feature games from the show, and the winner will receive a very special prize!
We will host adults (age 21+) in the auditorium to compete. These are silly, low-stakes games that require wit and creativity. By the end of the evening, one sole winner will emerge as the first ever Library Taskmaster Champion.
For a chance to participate, you will be given a number at the door. Our magic number picker will determine who plays what game. Five games featuring five different contestants per game will lead to a LIVE FINAL where the winners of the previous five tasks will compete for TASKMASTER GLORY.
Registration is appreciated to receive an email reminder, cancellation link, and program updates. Seating is first come first served. Registration does not hold or guarantee a seat.
Questions? Email jjamsky@dconc.gov or sgibson@dconc.gov.
*not affiliated with actual Taskmaster (unfortunately)
If you have any accessibility questions or accommodation needs, please contact Jennifer at Accessibility_Services@dconc.gov. We ask that, if possible, all accommodation requests be made at least three (3) business days prior to the event.
