Kids 8 - 10 years old must be accompanied by an adult also taking the class.
This is a 1.5 hour one time class to try out the art of clay on a wheel. No experience needed.
In this Beginner class, we will already have your clay centered and ready to throw on the wheel. You will be instructed on how to make a basic cup and bowl.
Pricing: $65/person. This price includes the class and the bisque firing and glaze firing for ONE of the two pieces that you will make during the class. If you wish to keep your second piece it will be an additional $20 (paid for on the day of your class) which includes firing and glazing.
At the end of the class, you’ll choose your glaze color. You will leave your mug with us and we'll take care of the rest. We let it dry, then fire it once to bisque, glaze and fire it again.
