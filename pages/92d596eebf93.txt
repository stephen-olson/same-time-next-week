WHERE: Design Studio, The Scrap Exchange
Learn a new skill to increase your wardrobe or to make holiday gifts.
This series is designed for a sewing enthusiast that KNOWS how to operate a sewing machine. You don’t have to be an expert…. but you will need to know how to thread the sewing machine and the bobbin and sew a basic seam.
You will learn how to determine the size pattern needed for your size; the amount of fabric needed; and how to sew the simple pull-on pants. This 2-part sewing class is medium paced so that we can complete a pair of simple pull-on pants by the end of the second session.
