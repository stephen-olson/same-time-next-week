Intro To Simple Pull-On Pants
2 Part Pants Sewing class $80.00
Part 1
WHEN: Sunday, November 22, 2026, 6pm-8pm
WHERE: Make & Take Studio, The Scrap Exchange
Part 2
WHEN: Tuesday, November 24,2026 6pm-8pm
WHERE: Design Studio, The Scrap Exchange
Learn a new skill to increase your wardrobe or to make holiday gifts.
This series is designed for a sewing enthusiast that KNOWS how to operate a sewing machine. You don’t have to be an expert…. but you will need to know how to thread the sewing machine and the bobbin and sew a basic seam.
You will learn how to determine the size pattern needed for your size; the amount of fabric needed; and how to sew the simple pull-on pants. This 2-part sewing class is medium paced so that we can complete a pair of simple pull-on pants by the end of the second session.
PART 1
Sunday, November 22, 2026, 6pm-8pm Make & Take Studio
We will prepare the fabric and pattern for sewing.
BRING: fabric scissors, straight pins, 3 or 4 yards of fabric (example: cotton, fleece, linen, chambray, etc... nothing heavy) The Scrap Exchange sells some fabric. You will need to purchase before class if needed. DO NOT BRING A SEWING MACHINE on Sunday
PART 2
Tuesday, November 24, 2026, 6pm-8pm Design Studio
We will be completing the pull-on pants.
