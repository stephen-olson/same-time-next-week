How should I prepare?
Please come prepared to work and be flexible and engaged with the needs for your build day!
It is very important to arrive on time. Familiarize yourself with your route from your home or office to the site in advance, and email volunteer@habitatraleigh.org if you have questions about how to find the site.
To familiarize yourself with basic safety and job site expectations, please watch this Habitat safety video.
Do I need to have construction experience to volunteer?
No, we will have staff and skilled volunteers on site to guide you to safely complete all the tasks for the day!
Safety is our highest concern. When you arrive, staff will provide safety instructions and training on how to perform tasks. If you feel uncomfortable with a task or tool, be sure to ask for help or ask to be re-assigned to a different task.
We value quality over speed. Relax and have fun! We are happy to teach you!
What's the workday schedule?
8:00–8:10 am: Volunteer check-in and registration (Please arrive promptly so we can begin on time! For safety reasons late-comers may be turned away.)
8:15 am: Volunteer orientation and safety overview, tasks assigned, workday begins
11:30 am–12:00 pm: Lunch break (Lunch is not provided unless otherwise indicated.)
3:00–3:30 pm: Site Cleanup

What should I bring?
Habitat Greater Raleigh provides all necessary tools and safety equipment for your workday! If you choose to bring your own gear (such as work gloves, hammer, tape measure, tool belt or nail apron, pencil) label any tools you bring with your name.
We will have big jugs of water and cups on site, so please bring your own water bottle. We will have sunscreen and bug spray as well, but if you have sensitive skin, it is advisable that you bring your own products. When is lunch?
Please bring lunch (or plan for lunches to be delivered/available for your team) and a cooler, if needed. Everyone will break for lunch from 11:30 a.m. to 12:15 pm. If you plan on having someone deliver lunch to you or your group on site, please ask for delivery by 11:15 am.
What is the bathroom situation?
There are portable toilets (porta-potties) on every site. These toilets are regularly cleaned to provide, and we have handwashing stations available on site as well.
What is the minimum age for volunteers?
Construction volunteers must be 16 years or older. Any volunteers under the age of 18 will need to enter a parent or guardian’s email to have a parental consent waiver confirmed digitally.
What should I wear?
Wear good work shoes – boots are best, sturdy sneakers are fine. No sandals, open‐toed or open-heeled shoes or light canvas-type shoes. Wear clothes that can get dirty. If you must complete court-ordered or lawyer-recommended community service hours or are completing community service leading up to a court hearing, these cannot be completed on the construction sites. These requests must be completed at our ReStore locations.
