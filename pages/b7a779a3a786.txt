Durham Community Concert Band
Welcome to the Durham Community Concert Band! For over 40 years, this non-profit, community-centered concert band has entertained and supported Durham, North Carolina and neighboring communities. Read on to find out how you can join us or come out and see us!
Rehearsals
Rehearsals are open to all, without audition. A few easy steps:
Email us at info@durhamband.org with your name and instrument.
Join us for your first rehearsal, bringing your instrument (unless a percussionist) and music stand. You may read music off a neighbor in your section
If you choose to continue, you will be added to our listserv and be given directions on how to access our music via Google Drive.
Have fun!
