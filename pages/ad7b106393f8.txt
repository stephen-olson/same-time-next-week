About
Community first, miles second. Downtown Raleigh's nonprofit run club.
URC is a 501(c)(3) built on health, wellness, and community through the joy of running. We celebrate Black culture and excellence while welcoming runners and walkers of every level. And yes, we welcome folks with all backgrounds and identities. No pace requirements, no experience needed.
We run once a week in or around Downtown Raleigh, rotating between our favorite spots, and we stay for the social: dancing, conversation, events, local partners, good people.
RSVP to events and check in when you arrive. It directly supports what URC can offer this community. Routes live at urbanrunclubraleigh.com.
