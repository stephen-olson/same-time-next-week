About
Pullen Dance is the longest running ballroom dance community in North Carolina.
We’re a small, friendly group that sponsors ballroom dancing socials at Pullen Park Community Center in Raleigh. Adults of all dance skills and abilities are welcome to attend. Click here to learn more about us. Everyone is welcome!
Dance Information
When: Every Wednesday night from 7:00–9:00 p.m.
Where: Pullen Park Community Center, 408 Ashe Ave, Raleigh, NC 27607
Details and admission: The first and third Wednesday of each month are $2 and feature a DJ and dance lesson; the lesson starts at 7:15 p.m. If you have tunes you'd like to hear, complete our contact us form.
