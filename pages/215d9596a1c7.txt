Jam Session
This jam, held on the 3rd Monday of every month, is open to all musicians, not only beginners! But it’s a learning jam to help those who are still learning the basics of bluegrass music and playing with other musicians. This is a great opportunity for participants of all ages and abilities to learn …
Facilitator Jef Walter is a local musician who plays with the Beer & Banjos All-Stars and other groups; he also mentors The Loblollies youth bluegrass band.

Additional Venue Information
Harry's Guitar Shop opened in 1985 in Raleigh. Since then, they have consistently provided top notch gear, service, and instruction to all types of musicians, from professionals to first-time buyers.
In addition to their extensive selection of instruments, they keep a wide variety of strings, straps, tubes, pickups and other accessories on hand.
They also offer a variety of instructional books, including popular artists songbooks and guides for the entire range of instruments they sell.
They are also a full service guitar shop. They keep a full inventory of guitar parts in stock, and their resident guitar-tech provides expert repair and service, ensuring structural integrity and proper playability for your instrument.
